Wyrdbound is a Norse-mythology roguelike hack-and-slash in the spirit of *Hades*, built in Unity 6. It is a side project that I build with AI assistance (Claude Code). The first chapter is called *Saga I: The Bound Son*.

You play Narfi, son of Loki, who wakes dead in the hall of his half-sister Hel. Each escape climbs through four realms of the dead toward the cave where his father lies bound, and every death returns you to Hel's hall, where the story moves forward and you grow stronger.

## What is in the game

- **Fast combat** with dashes, hit-stop and knockback into walls
- **Four weapons:** an axe, a spear, a bow and twin seaxes
- **58 blessings** from seven gods, each god with its own signature mechanic
- **Four realms** with procedurally generated chambers, 20 enemy types, 3 mini-bosses and 4 bosses
- **A hub** where you talk to the dead, give gifts and read a codex
- **Permanent upgrades** and optional difficulty modifiers
- **A branching story** with about 180 conversations and an ending that sets up Saga II

Almost everything is built in code: the floors, icons, interface, every sound effect and the whole generative Nordic score are produced by the game itself. The 3D characters and props are free CC0 models.

## Try it

The installer runs on Windows 10 or 11 and does not need Unity. Download it from the [releases page](https://github.com/EladOzCohen/Wyrdbound/releases/latest), or see the code on [GitHub](https://github.com/EladOzCohen/Wyrdbound).
